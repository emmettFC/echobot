## Acknowledgements

This work draws on prior MATLAB operating code by the MIT/WHOI EchoBot team. The EK80 pipeline's pulse-compression steps are a faithful reimplementation of [echopype](https://github.com/OSOceanAcoustics/echopype)'s `ek80_complex` module, which itself implements the algorithms described in:

> Demer, D. A., Andersen, L. N., Bassett, C., Berger, L., Chu, D., Condiotty, J., Cutter, G. R. Jr., Hutton, B., Korneliussen, R., Le Bouffant, N., Macaulay, G., Michaels, W. L., Murfin, D., Pobitzer, A., Renfree, J. S., Sessions, T. S., Stierhoff, K. L., and Thompson, C. H. (2017). 2016 USA–Norway EK80 Workshop Report: Evaluation of a wideband echosounder for fisheries and marine ecosystem science. *ICES Cooperative Research Report No. 336.*
